# Notion

Notion is a cloud-based productivity tool that combines notes, databases, wikis, and project management. While it supports some [[Knowledge Management]] workflows, it lacks native [[Bidirectional Links]] in the `[[wikilink]]` sense.

## Limitations for Zettelkasten

- No true backlinks (mentions exist but are limited)
- Cloud-only — no offline access without workarounds
- Database-centric rather than note-centric
- Encourages [[Folder-based Organization]] over networked thinking

## When Notion Works

Notion excels at structured project management and team wikis. For personal [[Zettelkasten]] practice, [[Obsidian]] or [[Logseq]] are more suitable.
