# Elaborative Encoding

Elaborative encoding is a cognitive science principle: you remember information better when you connect it to existing knowledge and express it in your own words.

## Why It Matters for Zettelkasten

The [[Zettelkasten]] method leverages elaborative encoding in two ways:

1. **Rewriting** — [[Atomic Notes]] must be written in your own words, not copied
2. **Linking** — Creating [[Bidirectional Links]] forces you to think about how new information relates to what you already know

This is why simply saving bookmarks or clipping articles doesn't constitute [[Knowledge Management]].

## Research Basis

Studies in cognitive psychology consistently show that:
- Generating information (writing) beats passive review (reading)
- Connecting new information to prior knowledge improves retention
- Spaced retrieval practice reinforces memory
