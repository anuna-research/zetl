# GTD (Getting Things Done)

GTD is a productivity methodology by David Allen. While primarily focused on task management rather than [[Knowledge Management]], it shares the principle of externalizing mental load.

## The Five Steps

1. **Capture** — collect everything into an inbox
2. **Clarify** — decide what each item means and what to do
3. **Organize** — put items in the right place
4. **Reflect** — review regularly
5. **Engage** — take action

## Comparison with Zettelkasten

GTD and [[Zettelkasten]] operate in different domains:

- GTD manages **actions** (tasks, projects, commitments)
- Zettelkasten manages **knowledge** (ideas, concepts, insights)

They complement each other. You might use GTD for "write a blog post about X" and [[Zettelkasten]] for the actual ideas that go into that post.
