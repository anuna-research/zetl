# Progressive Summarization

Progressive Summarization is a technique by Tiago Forte for distilling notes in layers:

1. **Layer 0** — Original full text
2. **Layer 1** — Bold the most important passages
3. **Layer 2** — Highlight within the bolded passages
4. **Layer 3** — Executive summary in your own words
5. **Layer 4** — Remix into original output

## Connection to Zettelkasten

While [[Progressive Summarization]] is associated with the [[Second Brain]] framework, it complements the [[Zettelkasten]] workflow well. The final layers (3-4) are essentially [[Atomic Notes]] — distilled ideas in your own words.

The key insight shared with [[Elaborative Encoding]]: rewriting forces deeper processing than passive highlighting.
