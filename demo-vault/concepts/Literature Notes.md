# Literature Notes

Literature notes are notes taken while reading or consuming a source (book, article, video). They capture the source's ideas in your own words, with references to the original.

## Role in the Zettelkasten Workflow

In [[Niklas Luhmann]]'s [[Zettelkasten]], literature notes are an intermediate step:

1. **Fleeting notes** — quick captures during reading
2. **Literature notes** — organized summaries of a source's key ideas
3. **[[Atomic Notes]]** — permanent, rewritten insights linked to the network

The critical step is moving from literature notes to atomic notes. Without this, you're just collecting — see the Collector's Fallacy in [[Knowledge Management]].

## Best Practices

- Write in your own words (see [[Elaborative Encoding]])
- Include the bibliographic reference
- Mark ideas that deserve their own [[Atomic Notes]]
- Process literature notes within 24-48 hours
