# Evergreen Notes

Evergreen notes are a concept developed by Andy Matuschak. They are notes that are continuously refined and updated, designed to accumulate value over time.

## Principles

1. **Evergreen notes should be atomic** — same as [[Atomic Notes]]
2. **Evergreen notes should be concept-oriented** — titled by the idea, not the source
3. **Evergreen notes should be densely linked** — via [[Bidirectional Links]]
4. **Prefer associative ontologies over hierarchical** — see [[Emergent Structure]]

## Relationship to Zettelkasten

Evergreen notes are essentially a modern restatement of [[Zettelkasten]] principles. The key difference is emphasis: Matuschak focuses on the iterative refinement of notes, while Luhmann's system emphasized the conversation between notes.

Both share the core insight that [[Atomic Notes]] connected by [[Bidirectional Links]] create a system more valuable than its parts.
