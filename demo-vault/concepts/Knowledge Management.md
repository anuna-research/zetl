# Knowledge Management

Knowledge management (KM) is the practice of capturing, organizing, and retrieving information to support learning and decision-making.

## Personal Knowledge Management

Personal KM (PKM) focuses on the individual. Key approaches include:

- [[Zettelkasten]] — Niklas Luhmann's interlinked note system
- [[Second Brain]] — Tiago Forte's CODE framework (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express)
- [[GTD]] — David Allen's Getting Things Done (more task-focused)

## Digital vs. Analog

Traditional KM used physical filing systems and [[Folder-based Organization]]. Modern PKM tools like [[Obsidian]], [[Logseq]], and [[Notion]] enable [[Bidirectional Links]] and full-text search, making networked thinking practical.

## The Collector's Fallacy

A common pitfall: collecting information without processing it. Simply saving an article is not knowledge management. The [[Zettelkasten]] approach combats this by requiring you to write [[Atomic Notes]] in your own words.
