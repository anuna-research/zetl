# Niklas Luhmann

Niklas Luhmann (1927-1998) was a German sociologist and one of the most prolific academic writers of the 20th century. He published over 70 books and nearly 400 articles.

## The Zettelkasten

Luhmann attributed his extraordinary productivity to his [[Zettelkasten]], which he maintained from the 1950s until his death. The system contained approximately 90,000 handwritten index cards.

His physical system ([[Luhmann's Slip Box]]) used a unique numbering scheme that allowed branching sequences of ideas — a precursor to [[Bidirectional Links]] in digital tools.

## Key Ideas

- Systems theory and autopoiesis
- Communication as the fundamental unit of social systems
- The [[Zettelkasten]] as a "communication partner" — the system surprised him with connections he hadn't planned

## Legacy

Luhmann's note-taking method was rediscovered in the 2010s through the work of Soenke Ahrens ("How to Take Smart Notes") and the rise of tools like [[Obsidian]] that implement digital versions of the [[Zettelkasten]].
